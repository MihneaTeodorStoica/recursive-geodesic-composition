## Earlier full-repository mirror

An earlier mirror of the named public source was audited through an unauthenticated browser, raw-file interface and complete downloads. Text updates fixed split citation/BibTeX names and the institution, but that mirror still served personal log paths and identifying PNG metadata; rewritten release/preprint links did not host release assets. Its URL and raw identity evidence are intentionally absent from this review tree.

That historical failure is **not a claim that a new mirror of this pre-sanitized snapshot has failed**. A newly configured mirror must be verified separately against this tree's manifest, not assumed to follow a different branch. Inspect its actual README, source/configuration/result payloads, binary metadata, links and complete download before sharing its URL. Raw identity-bearing and publication audits remain author-only outside the branch.

The platform's public [FAQ](https://anonymous.4open.science/faq) says binary files are served as-is and describes an 8 MB file limit. The included binaries were sanitized/inspected before publication; text regex replacement is not their privacy guarantee. The largest JSON file and all included probes must also be tested through actual downloads rather than inferred from the documented limit.
