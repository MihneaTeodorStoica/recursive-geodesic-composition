# Evidence and scope of the reviewed manuscript

The frozen `official-v6` result and its 4,000 selected prefixes are preserved. This is an **additive post-review analysis**, not a fresh confirmatory test. New models in the last study were trained on a disjoint exact-token TRAIN selection, but their safe-arm evaluation reuses the already evaluated 4,000 documents. The independent LongEmbed task corpora add downstream evidence; the method and tasks were chosen during revision, not preregistered before the original experiments. All intervals are conditional, not simultaneous across analyses or tasks.

## Scientific and reproducibility qualifications

- The held-out TRAIN selection initially targeted 500 fit + 100 validation per corpus; insufficient eligible FineWeb exact-four documents in the fixed source window required a **pre-encoding** recovery to 160 + 40 per corpus. Failures, original configuration, and recovery are preserved under `reproducibility/protocols/review_completion_v1/`. No validation-based model selection occurred.
- The LongEmbed FP16 masked-mean accumulator overflowed for two context-document references before any retrieval score existed. The recovery uniformly uses FP32 accumulation for **all** mean-based leaf, query and context embeddings with FP16 backbone weights unchanged. NarrativeQA required physical JSONL line parsing; completed task outputs were verified unchanged. Both failures remain documented.
- Official, safe baseline, binary reuse, reconstruction-calibration and source/final result JSON bytes are unchanged. Earlier reported FineWeb AUC and OpenWebText reconstruction tolerance exceedances remain visible in their original reports.
- `results/*_v1/ARTIFACT_HASHES.json` records local output identities; large derived vectors and caches are deliberately excluded from Git, with exact expected checksums. New TRAIN-fitted model/probe weights are separately identified in `reproducibility/REVIEW_RELEASE_MANIFEST.json`. Previously released source/final checkpoints and candidate probes are in the immutable `v1-paper` release; new artifacts must not be relabeled as historical ones.
- One teacher model, English-only training data, reused safe documents, three inherited source initializations and limited tasks restrict generalization. Exact-token overlap checks do not exclude near duplicates. Retrieval results compare frozen vector interfaces, not from-scratch or matched-CLS systems. Performance at 96+ leaves is *executed and ranked* on LongEmbed, not directly teacher-validated.
- Next controlled steps would require multiple fresh task corpora, a CLS-trained merger, matched-budget complete source lineages, geometry/arity controls, and a loss-removal comparison. None is represented as completed here.
