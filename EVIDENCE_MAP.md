# Evidence map for manuscript Tables 4–17

## Scope and reading the map

This map follows the table and figure numbering in the source snapshot’s `paper/main.tex`; paths below are relative to the supplement root. `\input` tables are called out explicitly because their table bodies live outside the manuscript source. The source manuscript is the authority for the claims and numbering; it need not be included in the supplementary ZIP.

“Offline” below means reviewing the frozen evidence, typesetting a tracked `.tex` fragment, or re-aggregating saved scalar scores. It does **not** mean rerunning the neural inference or recreating model embeddings. A result JSON/report is a record of a completed analysis, not a fresh evaluation.

## Offline evidence, missing prerequisites, and training-lineage boundary

- **Available without inference:** all tracked result JSON/Markdown, the five literal table fragments for Tables 4, 8, 10, 13, and 15, the saved figures for Figures 2–4, and table/figure renderers for the recursive-reuse, calibration, and continuation namespaces. Figure 1 is generated in TeX/TikZ. The inline manuscript tables and validation summaries are already source text/JSON; their presence is not a claim of a fresh evaluation.
- **Scalar-score re-aggregation:** where per-document/query score arrays are listed above, paired contrasts, intervals, and ECDFs can be checked from those stored values with the frozen protocol. This still cannot replay the model, independently reconstruct all inputs, or yield missing vectors.
- **Weights:** the source snapshot contains no tracked `.pt`/safetensors assets. The anonymous ZIP includes six exact historical fitted parent-only probe tensors—three `candidate_parent_only_fitted_probe` and three `source_parent_only_fitted_probe` artifacts—under `weights/` (about 47.3 MB total). They are not model checkpoints. `reproducibility/WEIGHTS_MANIFEST.json` gives their included paths and exact identities. No model checkpoints are included; the remaining 60 large model/new-probe assets (8,062,738,309 bytes) are external to meet the 100 MB ZIP cap. The manifest is not a binary payload. No publicly anonymous mirror has been established; if needed, request/provide the exact external filenames and SHA-256 digests from the sanitized manifests through the anonymous submission discussion. No identifying download URL is given here.
- **Caches/data:** the final clean TRAIN and official caches, derived teacher/leaf tensors, raw corpus text, and task embeddings are not source-tree assets. Their identities/regeneration requirements are declared in the manifests and the study protocols. Regenerating them needs the pinned upstream model/data, network access, relevant dataset/model terms, and compatible GPU/software. Official full-document processing must not be presented as having teacher-root targets.
- **Continuation versus historical source training:** clean-v6 final checkpoints continue inherited merger weights, reinitialize the decoder, and run 300 decoder-only plus 100 joint updates. This is not training from scratch. Available source/final checkpoint identities and the continuation schedule do **not** establish the complete source-merger training recipe or total historical training budget. The new attached/detached gradient control is a separate exact-four TRAIN intervention, not a recreation of that lineage.
- **Numerical scope:** safe-prefix bootstrap intervals condition on fixed fitted checkpoints; repeated checkpoint scores are not independent training runs. Recursive reuse changes tree/arity and applies to 606 teacher-valid exact-four prefixes; full-document runs have no direct teacher comparison. Reconstruction scores are bounded-decoder/probe diagnostics. Continuation compares a bundle of updates and losses. Retrieval is four pinned tasks, not evidence of consistent across-task superiority. K-ary scaling is a validation-only system comparison with differing capacity and training history.

## Package helper

The included `supplement.py` uses only the Python standard library. These commands were checked against its argument parser and implementation; run them from an extracted supplement copy:

```text
python supplement.py verify
python supplement.py report
python supplement.py materialize weights --role candidate_parent_only_fitted_probe --role source_parent_only_fitted_probe --mode hardlink
```

`verify` checks packaged files against `reproducibility/PACKAGE_MANIFEST.json` by size and SHA-256; it is not a signature check. `report` recomputes saved safe-prefix pooled means and paired means from 4,000 scalar rows and replays the 120 AUC point estimates in 60 saved classifier-prediction files. It prints the historical official AUC as a copied aggregate; it does not recompute bootstrap intervals or run inference, fitting, or training. `materialize` verifies the six included probe payloads against `reproducibility/WEIGHTS_MANIFEST.json` and places them at protocol lookup paths; it does not download or evaluate a model. Use `--verify-only` to check selected assets without placing them.

## Independent rebuild audit

The rebuild preserved all original scientific source modules and historical numeric JSON values, independently replayed the saved scalar summaries/AUCs and baseline bootstrap intervals, and investigated each inherited source-hash mismatch. See [VALIDATION.md](VALIDATION.md) and [HASH_DISCREPANCY_AUDIT.md](HASH_DISCREPANCY_AUDIT.md). These checks do not create missing embeddings, resolve the unidentified preparer bytes, provide external model access or establish the original source-checkpoint training lineage.
